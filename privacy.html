Website privacy notice

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This notice explains how CO-UNITY GROUP LLC ("CoUnity Data", "we", "us") handles information when you visit counity.tech. It covers this website only. Datasets we license are governed by separate licensing agreements.

Information we collect

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers, or contact forms, and it does not ask you to create an account. Fonts and other assets are served from this website, not from third-party font services.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically process technical information such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested, in order to deliver and secure the site.

When you contact us

If you email us at vlad@counity.tech, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your name, company, email address, and project details. We use it to respond to your inquiry, prepare samples or quotes, and manage any resulting business relationship. We do not sell this information.

Scheduling a call

Our "Book a call" links open Calendly, a third-party scheduling service. Information you enter there is processed by Calendly under its own privacy policy and shared with us so we can hold the meeting.

Retention and your choices

We keep correspondence for as long as needed for the purposes above and our legitimate business records. You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information you have sent us by emailing vlad@counity.tech.

Changes

We may update this notice from time to time. The date above shows when it was last revised.
