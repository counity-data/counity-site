Data quality & de-identification

Reviewed by medical professionals, case by case

Data is collected in clinical practice and goes through manual review before it is offered for licensing.

01 · CAPTURE

In-person consultation

Photographs are taken during the visit, and the treating dermatologist records the diagnosis.

02 · STRUCTURE

One case per patient

Collection is designed around one case per unique patient, with residual duplication estimated below 1%.

03 · REVIEW

Manual medical QC

Each case is checked for duplicates, image quality, age and sex accuracy, and de-identification.

04 · PROTECT

De-identified delivery

Records carry de-identified IDs, not names or contact details, and are licensed only under a signed agreement.

HIPAA-aligned de-identification. Our documented patient de-identification process is aligned with HIPAA Safe Harbor methods. We are happy to walk your compliance team through it during scoping.
